# Roadmap

## Done

**0.1** First release: integration with storage and WebSocket API, sidebar panel,
planner with rooms, snapping, undo, area import and background image, library with
devices, doors, windows, passages and furniture, 3D view with live devices, room
colours and symbols, 2D live map for devices without WebGL 2, English and German.

**0.2** Animations: warm air above heating radiators, fresh air at open windows, pulses
at motion sensors, sound waves at speakers, TV picture and driving robot vacuum. A
switch per device, a capped frame rate and CSS animations in the 2D live map.

**0.3** TV screen with app or source, title and playback state.

**0.4** Walls towards the camera are lowered automatically. Double tap focuses a room.

**0.5** More furniture and wall snapping, radiators with adjustable length, tapping a
room zooms in or shows only that room.

**0.6** Live sync between open panels with a notice on conflicts. The wall behind a TV
stays up.

**0.7** Adjustable limits for temperature, humidity and CO₂.

**0.8** Room colours by humidity, CO₂ or heating state. App icons and artwork on the TV.

**0.9** Dashboard card.

**0.10** Labels behind walls are hidden, lamps show their real colour everywhere, plan
export and import, automated tests, security review, release workflow.

**0.11** Floors, a welcome screen with a demo home, uploading and scaling a background
image, a visual card editor, a list of missing entities, a room card in 3D and in the
2D live map, light and open doors in the 2D live map, Greek.

**0.12** 58 pieces of furniture in nine categories with search and a 3D preview in the
library, a switch for the automatic lowering of walls, and an optional tap to switch
lamps.

**0.13** Stairs up, stairs down and spiral stairs.

## Next
